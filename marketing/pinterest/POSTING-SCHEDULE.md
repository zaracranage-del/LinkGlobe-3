# Pinterest Posting Schedule

## Post order (1 per day)
| Day | Pin | Goal |
|-----|-----|------|
| Day 1 | pin-1-problem-hook | Virality, shares |
| Day 2 | pin-2-one-link | Click-throughs |
| Day 3 | pin-3-earnings | Saves, SEO |
| Day 4 | pin-4-step-by-step | Long-term saves |
| Day 5 | pin-5-relatable | Emotion, comments |

## Best posting times
8pm–11pm your local time
Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday perform best

## After posting all 5
- Check which pin has the most outbound clicks after 7 days
- Repin that one every week to a different board
- Make 3 variations of your best performer

## Boards to post to
Create these boards if you don't have them:
- "Affiliate Marketing Tips"
- "Make Money as a Creator"
- "Amazon Finds & Affiliate Links"
- "Creator Business Tips"
- "Passive Income Ideas"

Post each pin to at least 2 boards.

## Why you're getting views but no outbound clicks
- Add the URL (linkglobe.co) visibly ON the image itself
- Use "→ linkglobe.co" as a text element in Canva
- Pinterest users don't always read descriptions
- Pins 1 and 4 have the strongest CTAs — post those first

## Tracking
Add ?utm_source=pinterest to your destination URL:
https://linkglobe.co?utm_source=pinterest
This will show Pinterest clicks separately in your LinkGlobe analytics.
